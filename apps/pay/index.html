Are future updates included?

Yes. Every future update to Bridgistic is included in the Lifetime Deal.

How do I activate my license?

After purchase you receive a license key by email. Paste it on the License screen in the Bridgistic dashboard or plugin. The license is verified by the WPistic license server.

How many sites can I use it on?

Lifetime Deal: 1 license · 1 site. Monthly and Yearly: 1 license · 3 sites. A site is one production site with the license activated; local and staging copies do not count.
