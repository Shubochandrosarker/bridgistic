Questions

What does the lifetime offer include?

One payment of $69 gives one license for one site, all current Pro features, and future Bridgistic updates. It has no renewal.

Which provider processes payment?

Stripe is the default checkout for Bridgistic. Paddle remains available when you choose it. The checkout shows applicable taxes before payment; WordPressistic LLC is the seller for Stripe transactions.

Can I get a refund?

Every purchase includes a 7-day refund guarantee. After 7 days, discretionary refunds are not available, but applicable-law, duplicate, unauthorized, provider-error and valid chargeback cases remain reviewable. A full refund ends the associated license. Contact support@wpistic.com so the payment provider and license ledger stay synchronized.

What happens if a recurring payment fails?

You receive a 7-day grace period to resolve the payment. If it remains unresolved, the paid entitlement may expire or be suspended until billing is current.

How do I connect my site?

Install the Bridgistic WordPress plugin, create a scoped key, and connect it at dashboard.bridgistic.app/sites.
