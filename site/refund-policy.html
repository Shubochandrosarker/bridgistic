Refund Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026. Operated by WordPressistic LLC, doing business as WPistic.

Every Bridgistic purchase comes with a 7-day refund guarantee. Bridgistic Pro is sold by WordPressistic LLC when Stripe is selected, with Paddle available as an optional merchant-of-record checkout. The WPistic Refund Policy also applies.

1. The 7-day guarantee

If Bridgistic is not right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of your purchase and you receive it in full, with no questions asked. The guarantee covers the Lifetime Deal (US$69.00), the monthly plan (US$12.00) and the yearly plan (US$99.00).

The provider used for checkout issues the refund to your original payment method. Stripe or Paddle timing is normally 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

2. Monthly and yearly plans after 7 days

You can cancel a monthly or yearly renewal at any time and keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. After the 7-day guarantee, discretionary refunds and prorated refunds are not available.

We will still review and honor refunds, adjustments or reversals required by applicable consumer law, duplicate or unauthorized transactions, provider errors, and valid card-network disputes or chargebacks. Contact support promptly so the payment provider and license record can be reconciled.

3. What a refund or cancellation means for your license

A full refund ends and revokes the associated license, whether the refund is issued during the 7-day guarantee or as a permitted legal, provider-error, duplicate, unauthorized-transaction or chargeback resolution. We may end a license by hand after the provider confirms the refund, rather than automatically, so it is not always immediate.

Cancellation stops future renewal; it does not create a discretionary refund for time already paid. Access continues through the paid period unless the provider or law requires an earlier reversal.

4. Failed payments

If a recurring payment fails, the subscription enters a 7-day grace period while we and the payment provider retry or resolve the payment. Access remains available during that grace period. If the payment is still unresolved after 7 days, the paid entitlement expires and the associated license is suspended until billing is brought current.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email support@wpistic.com from the address you bought with, or include it in your message, and tell us which purchase it is. The WPistic Refund Policy aims for a reply within 2 business days.

6. Exceptions

The exceptions in section 6 of the WPistic Refund Policy, for abuse and fraud, still apply.
