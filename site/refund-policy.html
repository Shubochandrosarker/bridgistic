Refund Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026. Operated by WordPressistic LLC, doing business as WPistic.

Every Bridgistic purchase comes with a 7-day refund guarantee. Bridgistic Pro is licensed by WPistic, and the WPistic Refund Policy also applies. Refunds are processed by the provider used for the purchase.

1. The 7-day guarantee

If Bridgistic is not right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of your purchase and you receive it in full, with no questions asked. The guarantee covers the Lifetime Deal (US$69), the monthly plan (US$12.00) and the yearly plan (US$99.00).

Paddle issues refunds for Paddle purchases as merchant of record. WordPressistic LLC issues refunds for Stripe purchases through Stripe. Refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

2. After 7 days

After the 7-day guarantee, refunds are not normally approved. You can cancel a monthly or yearly plan at any time and keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. You can turn off renewal from the provider or dashboard.

Exceptions may be made where required by law or in a documented support case at WordPressistic LLC's discretion. A provider may also process a payment correction or chargeback independently of this policy.

3. What a refund or cancellation means for your license

A refund ends the license, and so does a cancellation once the paid period is over. We may end a license by hand after the refund or cancellation is confirmed, rather than automatically, so it is not always immediate.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email support@wpistic.com from the address you bought with, or include it in your message, and tell us which purchase it is. The WPistic Refund Policy aims for a reply within 2 business days.

5. Exceptions

The exceptions in section 6 of the WPistic Refund Policy, for abuse and fraud, still apply.
